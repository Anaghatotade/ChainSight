# Data source

**Kaggle - M5 Forecasting - Accuracy** (Walmart US store sales). Competition page: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/m5-forecasting-accuracy

Reference: Makridakis, S., Spiliotis, E., Assimakopoulos, V. (2022). *The M5 accuracy competition: Results, findings and conclusions.* International Journal of Forecasting.

## Files used (put them in `data/raw/`)

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `calendar.csv` | one row per day: date, week id (`wm_yr_wk`), weekday, month, year, events, SNAP flags (CA / TX / WI) |
| `sales_train_validation.csv` (or `.csv.gz`) | unit sales per item-store, wide format, columns `d_1` ... `d_1913` (2011-01-29 to 2016-04-24) |
| `sell_prices.csv` (or `.csv.gz`) | weekly sell price per store and item (a missing row = the item was not on sale that week) |

`sales_train_evaluation.csv` and `sample_submission.csv` are **not** needed. Plain `.csv` and gzipped `.csv.gz` both work. The exact files behind the published results are listed (with MD5 checksums) in the README and in `outputs/tables/raw_files.csv` after step 01.

## Why the data is not in the repository

The files come from a Kaggle competition: downloading needs a Kaggle login and acceptance of the competition rules, and the rules restrict redistribution. `.gitignore` therefore excludes `data/raw/` and `data/processed/`; everything in `data/processed/` is regenerated by the pipeline.

## What the data does and does not contain

Contains: daily unit sales for 3,049 items in 3 categories (FOODS, HOUSEHOLD, HOBBIES) and 7 departments, in 10 stores in 3 states (CA, TX, WI); weekly prices; holidays / events; SNAP benefit days.

Does **not** contain: stock on hand, stock-outs, purchase orders, supplier or lead-time data, product cost, margin, promotions other than price changes. Consequently fill rate, stock-out rate, inventory turnover and days of inventory cannot be computed, and a week with zero sales cannot be told apart from a stock-out.
